<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="research-focus-title">
  <p class="section-label">Research focus</p>
  <h2 id="research-focus-title">Institutions, inequality, and political choice</h2>
  <p>My primary subfield is the political economy of inequality, democratic development, and clientelism. I also have a strong interest in statistical and experimental methods, including laboratory and survey-based research.</p>
  <ul class="research-themes" aria-label="Research themes">
    <li><span>01</span>Political economy of inequality</li>
    <li><span>02</span>Democratic development and clientelism</li>
    <li><span>03</span>Experimental and statistical methods</li>
  </ul>
  <p>My work is forthcoming or published in journals including <i>Electoral Studies</i>, <i>Political Psychology</i>, the <i>European Journal of Political Economy</i>, <i>Acta Politica</i>, and the <i>Journal of Politics in Latin America</i>.</p>
</section>
